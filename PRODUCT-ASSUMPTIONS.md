RE-PLAN
Re-Plan (inspired by Paradigm) is a site where the users can build their roadmaps that are interesting enough to keep them going.Here, the user initially answers few questions asked by the site and then accordingly the roadmap is prepared.It contains interesting things like deadlines with streaks, leagues and a place where you and your friend can together reach your goals together ,there will be cold streaks included but once they are also emptied, you will be demoted (this concept is inspired by duolingo).But guess what! You’ll have the few restore options every single month through which you can save yourself (inspired by snapchat). This will maintain the fun and will not let you be demotivated for not being consistent because there are times when you’ll miss your daily routine.
Students/others often face the problem of not being able to keep up with the normal plans the application made for them. And once if the discipline thing breaks off then they don’t feel like following it and also theer are times when they’d want to do the task but can’t do it because of other important plans/exams and they’ll miss their that particular day’s plan. This is where Re-Plan comes into picture helps them rearrange their plan accordingly again.
The target users are the students/others who do self study while preparing for exams or jobs or some other tasks and even the ones who are preparing for their academic exams.

The main use case are explained in the above paras i.e, Re-planning their schedules, Maintenance of curiosity(streaks,fun deadlines,restoring,leagues) , Knowledge of where they are right now(what they have finished and how much is still remaining)
Core features : Goals setter(setting goals,weekly goals), Progress tracking, Achievements(points,streaks,leagues,fun texts/notifications,learning together with friends,curiosity), Recovery(replanning things if they miss deadlines, restoring of streaks)
Users might start following things only for 2-3 days and then they get bored ,that’s why we plan to bring Re-Plan where the users can have fun while learning, users of duolingo have that burning fire discipline maintained because they have a thought in their mind of not breaking the streaks,we plan to make our users have the same thought while learning something.Small goals leads to bigger achievements.
My MVP includes setting goals,tracking their progress, replanning their goals if they missed it(limited recovery every month), recovering the streaks, checking out on deadlines and comparing the previous break goals and after break goals,connecting with friends to make it interesting.
My MVP doesn’t include teaching users the topics/courses,subscriptions,long set of quizzes,larger goals,ai-generated topics,learning platform,..
